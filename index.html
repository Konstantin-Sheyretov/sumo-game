Sumo Game

Rules

Each round has two phases.

Planning: one by one, each player secretly picks an action — move one square (↑ ↓ ← →) or stay — and a priority number from 1 to 4. Each number can be used once; after you have used all four you get them all back.

Acting: plans are revealed and carried out in priority order, 1 first. On a tie, whoever is further left on the track acts first. The track starts in a random order.

Moving into a pawn pushes it, and any pawns lined up behind it. A pawn pushed off the edge is out. Each time you push someone, you move one place left on the track. If a planned move would take you off the board when it is your turn to act, it does nothing.

The last pawn standing wins.
