Sumo Game

Rules

Each round has two phases.

Every pawn faces a direction (shown by its pointer). Each pawn starts facing the next pawn clockwise.

Planning: one by one, each player secretly picks a command and a priority number from 1 to 4. Commands: forward (move one square ahead), left / right (turn 90° that way, then move one square), or stay. Each priority number can be used once; after you have used all four you get them all back.

Acting: plans are revealed and carried out in priority order, 1 first. On a tie, whoever is further left on the track acts first. The track starts in a random order. In round 1 only, the player who acts last does not move.

Moving into a pawn pushes it, and any pawns lined up behind it. A pawn that is pushed or walks off the edge is out. Each time you push someone, you move one place left on the track.

The game ends after everyone has used all four priority tokens 5 times (20 rounds). The player furthest left on the track wins — or the last pawn standing, if only one is left earlier.
